# Illustrative UNEP-Aligned Use Cases

| Challenge | Technology | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Deforestation | Satellite + AI alerts | Near real-time detection of forest loss |
| Air pollution | Low-cost IoT sensor networks | Hyperlocal air quality data for policy |
| Illegal wildlife trade | AI image/audio recognition | Faster species identification, anti-poaching |
| Plastic pollution | Enzymatic recycling, digital tracking | Higher recycling rates, reduced ocean waste |
| Climate finance | Blockchain-based MRV | Transparent, verifiable carbon markets |
| Renewable transition | Smart grids, storage tech | Lower emissions, more stable energy supply |
